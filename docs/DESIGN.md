# Design System and Visual Direction

## 1. Brand

**Kashi Riverside Stay** is presented as a rich, cultural Varanasi stay with modern comfort. The design should feel welcoming, memorable, refined, and connected to the visual character of Kashi without becoming crowded or overly ornate.

## 2. Core Design Direction

- Rich and cultural
- Modern but warm
- Creative and distinctive
- Elegant rather than generic
- Comfortable and welcoming
- Culturally expressive through selected details

The visual language should combine heritage-inspired details with clean contemporary structure.

## 3. Color Palette

### Primary colors

- Brown: `#74342B`
- Cream: `#EDDBC2`
- Deep brown: `#391212`
- Baby blue accent: `#8FA4D1`

### Usage rules

- Cream is the main page background and primary light surface.
- Brown is used for feature sections, cards, buttons, and visual emphasis.
- Deep brown is used for strong contrast, footer surfaces, and important text.
- Baby blue is used sparingly for accents, selected states, highlights, and interactive details.
- The palette should remain warm and balanced; baby blue must not become a dominant color.

## 4. Logo and Favicon

- The supplied KRS logo is the official brand logo.
- The logo should appear in the website header and footer where appropriate.
- The same brand identity will be used for the favicon.
- For very small favicon sizes, a compact emblem/cropped mark derived from the supplied logo may be used so the temple, sun, river, and boat identity remains legible.
- The logo should not be distorted, stretched, or recolored outside the approved palette without approval.

The final logo asset will be added to the project assets during implementation.

## 5. Typography

Typography should be creative and memorable while remaining uniform throughout the website.

### Typeface system

- Display headings and hotel name: **Cormorant Garamond**
- Body text, navigation, forms, labels, buttons, and prices: **Manrope**

### Typography rules

- Use hierarchy, weight, size, and spacing to create variety rather than using many unrelated fonts.
- Cormorant Garamond should provide the cultural and editorial character.
- Manrope should keep booking information and interface content clear and readable.
- Use consistent heading levels across pages.
- Avoid decorative typography for long paragraphs, forms, policies, or payment instructions.

## 6. Layout and Section Rhythm

- Use alternating cream and brown sections to create visual rhythm.
- Keep the overall layout spacious and easy to scan.
- Use deep brown for high-contrast areas and the footer.
- Use rounded image corners consistently.
- Use visible borders and soft shadows on content cards.
- Keep decorative elements limited to key areas.
- Preserve clear separation between informational content and booking actions.

## 7. Header and Navigation

### Desktop

- Logo on the left
- Navigation links in the center
- **Book a Room** button on the right
- Header overlays the hero video where appropriate

### Mobile

- Logo remains visible in the header
- Navigation changes to a hamburger menu
- Menu opens as a clean, prominent panel
- **Book a Room** remains easy to find
- WhatsApp access remains available

Navigation should be consistent across the public website.

## 8. Hero Section

The Home page hero will use:

- Full-width background video
- Header positioned over the video
- Very subtle warm-brown overlay
- Centered hero text
- Minimal content only

Hero text:

> Culture in Every Stay

The hero will not include a supporting subtitle. It will include a prominent **Book a Room** button below the tagline.

The supplied video is a visual reference/asset to be added to the project during implementation. A suitable fallback image or video crop must be provided for mobile and reduced-motion situations.

## 9. Floating Actions

### Book a Room

- Persistent throughout the website
- Opens the dedicated Booking page
- Slightly rounded rectangular shape
- Strong contrast against the current section
- Subtle lift, glow, or highlight on hover

### WhatsApp

- Separate floating WhatsApp action may be used
- Supports long-stay enquiries and general contact
- Must not overlap the booking button or cover important content

Floating actions should be adapted for mobile touch interfaces.

## 10. Revolving Amenities Strip

The amenities strip will appear near the top of the Home page.

- Cream background using `#EDDBC2`
- Brown labels using `#74342B`
- Small, consistent line icons
- Continuous automatic movement at a slow, calm speed
- Seamless looping
- Touch-friendly behavior on mobile
- No large heading; keep the strip compact

Example items:

`AC • Wi-Fi • TV • Hot & Cold Water • Room Service • Housekeeping • 24-Hour Reception • Power Backup`

The animation must respect reduced-motion preferences.

## 11. Home Page Sections

The Home page will contain:

- Header/navigation
- Video hero
- Revolving amenities strip
- Room preview
- Services section
- Why-stay-with-us section
- Gallery preview
- Footer

The Home page will not contain separate sections for the booking process, Varanasi experience, guest trust, or a final call to action.

## 12. Room Cards

The Rooms section will display two large side-by-side cards:

- AC Room
- Non-AC Room

Each card should include:

- Large rounded image
- Room type
- Current displayed price
- Key facilities
- Extra mattress information
- View details or Book Now action

Cards stack vertically on mobile. Use soft shadows, visible borders, and a slight lift on hover.

Seasonal/admin pricing explanations belong in the single Policies area, not in the Rooms section.

## 13. Services Section

The Services section will appear after the revolving amenities strip on the Home page and will use elegant horizontal cards.

Services include:

- Parking
- Extra bedding
- Boat ride arrangements
- Ticket booking assistance
- Car rental assistance
- Scooty rental assistance
- Local shopping assistance, including saree shopping

Each card should have a short name, concise description, consistent visual marker or icon, and an **Available on request** indication where appropriate. Additional charges may apply.

## 14. Gallery

The Gallery section will use a horizontal image carousel.

- Rounded image corners
- High-quality hotel and room photographs
- Previous and next controls
- Touch/swipe support on mobile
- Smooth transitions
- Optional captions
- Full gallery action if required

The carousel should avoid excessive automatic movement that makes images difficult to view.

## 15. Footer

The footer will use:

- Deep-brown background: `#391212`
- Cream text
- Subtle cultural details
- Hotel logo or brand identity
- Navigation links
- Contact details when supplied
- WhatsApp access
- Compact square map section
- Get Directions link
- One **Policies** link
- Copyright information

The map should be useful without dominating the footer. The Policies link opens all policy content in one place rather than separate policy links.

## 16. Buttons and Cards

### Buttons

- Slightly rounded rectangular shape
- Consistent height and padding
- Brown, deep-brown, or cream variants
- Cream text on dark buttons
- Baby-blue used selectively for hover or selected states
- Subtle lift, glow, color shift, or highlight effect
- Clear focus state for keyboard users

### Cards

- Visible but refined borders
- Soft shadows
- Rounded corners
- Consistent internal spacing
- Slight hover lift on desktop
- No excessive decoration or heavy gradients

## 17. Motion and Interaction

The website should use polished, subtle motion:

- Smooth scrolling
- Gentle fade-in and slide-up section reveals
- Soft card hover movement
- Elegant image transitions
- Animated navigation underlines
- Button hover lift or glow
- Ripple or highlight feedback on click where appropriate
- Subtle cursor-following glow or custom cursor effect on desktop

Motion rules:

- Effects must not distract from booking actions.
- Animations must remain performant.
- Cursor effects must be disabled on touch devices.
- Reduced-motion preferences must be respected.
- Avoid motion that interferes with reading, forms, or payment submission.

## 18. Decorative Cultural Elements

Use cultural details only in key areas:

- Header
- Hero
- Room cards
- Services section
- Footer

Possible motifs:

- Temple-inspired line patterns
- River-wave curves
- Elegant arches
- Fine ornamental borders
- Minimal heritage marks

Decorative elements should support the content and never compete with the logo, room information, or booking actions.

## 19. Booking Page Visual Direction

The booking page will be a dedicated page rather than a modal or slide-in panel.

The visual flow will be:

`Booking Details → Amount Calculation → QR Advance Payment → Payment Reference Submission → Booking Status`

The page should feel focused and trustworthy while retaining the hotel’s cultural style. Payment instructions, QR imagery, transaction reference fields, and status messages must be especially clear and readable.

## 20. Responsive Design

- Design for mobile first, then expand for tablet and desktop.
- Hero video must have a mobile-safe crop or fallback image.
- Side-by-side room cards stack vertically on small screens.
- Horizontal carousels support touch gestures.
- Navigation uses a hamburger menu on mobile.
- Floating actions must not block content or form controls.
- Text remains readable against video and colored backgrounds.

## 21. Accessibility and Usability

- Maintain readable color contrast.
- Provide visible keyboard focus states.
- Use meaningful labels for icons and buttons.
- Provide alt text for meaningful images.
- Ensure motion can be reduced or disabled.
- Do not rely on color alone to communicate booking or payment status.
- Keep booking and payment instructions plain and easy to understand.
